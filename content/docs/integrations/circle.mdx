## Overview

<Callout type="info">
  **Planned.** The Circle channel is on the near-term roadmap and is not
  available yet. This page describes the intended two-stage rollout so you can
  plan for it; the steps below are not live.
</Callout>

Circle is where many course creators and paid communities live. When the integration ships it will roll out in two stages:

1. **Read-only.** AnswerLoops ingests posts and comments from your chosen spaces, runs them through the standard pipeline, and drops the drafted answer into your AnswerLoops inbox for a team member to review and paste back. Nothing posts to Circle automatically.
2. **Automatic replies.** Once Circle's write API limits are confirmed for the relevant plan tiers, high-confidence answers post back into the thread directly, the same as Discord or Slack.

## How it will connect

| You provide | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Circle API token | **Settings → API** in your Circle admin |
| Watched spaces | Chosen in AnswerLoops settings after connecting |

## Plan availability

Circle will be a Standard-plan-and-above channel, gated the same way as Slack and GitHub.
